# AURORA data card

Every number AURORA reports comes from a **digital twin**. Real station telemetry is not public, so the prototype combines real weather with physics-based synthetic loads. This card lists every source and assumption so the results can be checked and replaced with NCPOR data.

## 1. Weather (real)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Source | NASA POWER hourly API (`community=RE`, MERRA-2 based), `power.larc.nasa.gov` |
| Points | Bharati −69.41, 76.19 · Maitri −70.77, 11.73 · Himadri (Ny-Ålesund) 78.92, 11.93 |
| Years | 2021 (train), 2022 (calibration/validation), 2023 (test and year simulation) |
| Variables | T2M, WS10M, WS50M, ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN, CLRSKY_SFC_SW_DWN, PS, RH2M |
| Resolution | hourly, linearly interpolated to 15 min |
| Files | `data/raw/<station>_nasa_power_2021_2023.csv` plus a `.json` provenance file |

Derived fields:
- **Blizzard flag:** 10 m wind ≥ 15 m/s.
- **Panel snow cover:** drops to 35% during blizzards and recovers 2.5%/h.
- **Station clock:** UTC offset from the config.

If the download fails, `twin/weather.py` falls back to a seeded synthetic generator. That generator has a seasonal cycle, AR(1) anomalies, katabatic diurnal wind and stochastic blizzards.

**Known limitation:** reanalysis smooths local katabatic peaks, so real stations see more extreme gusts than the data shows. The blizzard event injection exists to test those extremes.

## 3. Loads (synthetic, physics-based)

Generated by `twin/loads.py` at 15-minute steps.

- **Crew calendar:** 24 people in winter, about 70 in summer (15 Nov – 28 Feb). Summer crew changes ±6 week to week.
- **Electric load by priority tier:**
  - **Tier 1 (life support):** ventilation (10 kW + 0.15 kW per person), medical and fire safety (4 kW), galley at meals (0.45 kW per person). Water production is a daily energy budget: 300 kWh + 9 kWh per person, at most 120 kW, and must finish within the local day.
  - **Tier 2 (comms and safety):** 12 kW.
  - **Tier 3 (science):** 14 kW base plus about 3 energy-heavy experiments a week (20–40 kW for 3–6 h), scheduled in advance.
  - **Tier 4 (comfort):** 0.55 kW per active person, plus laundry at 3 kWh per person per week (deferrable).
- **Heat demand:** UA 3.4 kW/K × (20 °C − outdoor) × (1 + 0.02 × wind), scaled by occupancy, plus hot water at 0.35 kW per person. 70% of heat is Tier 1 (habitable minimum).
- **Noise:** about 3% AR(1) multiplicative noise.

**Known limitation:** synthetic loads follow their generating equations, so forecasts are more accurate than they would be on real meters. Expect higher error on real data. The PRD target (MAPE < 10%) leaves room for that.

## 4. Forecasts

- **Weather forecast:** synthesised from the realised weather, issued as a new run every 6 hours like a real weather centre. Re-plans between runs reuse the same forecast. Errors are autocorrelated and grow with time since the run was issued:
  - temperature σ = 0.5 + 0.06·h °C;
  - log-wind σ = 0.08 + 0.006·h;
  - clear-sky index σ = 0.05 + 0.004·h.
- **Load and heat models:** gradient-boosted quantile regression (scikit-learn `HistGradientBoostingRegressor`, the same tree method as LightGBM; LightGBM's macOS build needs a Homebrew OpenMP library) for P10, P50 and P90.
  - Features: calendar, meals, crew, scheduled experiments, forecast temperature, wind and wind chill, load 48 h and 168 h earlier, and lead time.
- **PV and wind:** physics model on the forecast weather, plus a quantile ML correction.
- **Uncertainty bands:** split-conformal calibration on 2022, so the P10–P90 band covers about 80% of outcomes.
- **Validation:** train 2021 → calibrate 2022 → test 2023. Never shuffled. Full report in `docs/forecast_report_bharati.json`.

| Test year 2023 (Bharati) | Result |
|---|---|
| Load MAPE, 24 h ahead | 2.0% (persistence 7.6%) |
| Heat MAPE, 24 h ahead | 5.3% (persistence 8.5%) |
| Load / heat P10–P90 coverage | 80% / 76% |
| PV / wind nMAE, 24 h ahead | 1.8% / 11.1% of capacity |

## 5. Baseline (diesel-first)

This is how stations are typically run today (`twin/baseline.py`):
- The lead generator is always on.
- Extra units start to keep 20% spinning reserve.
- Renewables are used first.
- The battery buffers to keep generators at minimum load.
- Waste heat is used only when it coincides with demand (no thermal storage).
- Surplus renewables are curtailed.
- Water production starts at 09:00 and laundry at 10:00.

AURORA and the baseline run on the same twin, weather, loads and events. Each simulated month starts both from the same state (battery 60%, tank 50%).

## 6. Fuel Survival Score

- **Scenarios:** 1,000. Each future day takes a historical weather day: 5-day blocks, same season ±15 days, any of the three years.
- **Ship delay:** 60% chance the ship is on time; otherwise an exponential delay with a 7-day mean, capped at 30 days.
- **Daily fuel model:** a linear surrogate (electric kWh, heat kWh, renewable kWh available) fitted to the twin's year run of each strategy, with bootstrapped residuals.
- **Other fuel use:** vehicles and incinerator use 150 L/day, ×1.6 in summer, the same for both strategies.
- **Score:** the share of scenarios in which fuel never drops below the reserve before the ship arrives.

## 7. Load priority in an energy emergency

When there is not enough energy for everything (for example every generator tripped and the battery nearly empty), loads are cut in this order, in both the optimiser and the twin's emergency logic:
1. Power-to-heat.
2. Laundry.
3. Tier 4 (comfort).
4. Tier 3 (science).
5. Tier 2 (comms and safety).
6. Postponed water production.
7. Tier 1, only for the energy that physically does not exist.

`tests/test_tier1_protection.py` checks this at both levels.

## 8. What is not modelled

- Electrical transients below 15 minutes.
- Battery degradation beyond a linear wear cost.
- Generator thermal state (cold starts are covered by the start cost).
- Precipitation and snow-drift engineering.
- Real station control logic.
- Federated learning, the storm classifier and the health monitor (PRD P1/P2).

All results are **simulated** and must not be presented as measured savings at a real station.
